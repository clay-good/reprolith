How each class’s blind verdicts matched independently-established ground truth. An abstention (a “blocked” verdict — insufficient information) is shown apart from a wrong verdict; no single blended rate conflates them.

What these numbers do and do not establish. Where a class’s entries all carry the same expected verdict, “always answer that verdict” would score the same, so the number is evidence of agreement with an independent implementation and of abstaining when the evidence is missing — not of telling a reproducible result from an irreproducible one.
