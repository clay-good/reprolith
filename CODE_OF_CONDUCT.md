# Code of Conduct

Reprolith is a place for careful, honest, collaborative science. We ask everyone who takes part —
in issues, pull requests, verifications, and discussion — to hold to a few simple commitments.

## Our standards

- **Be respectful.** Assume good faith. Critique ideas and values, not people.
- **Be precise and honest.** Cite sources. Distinguish what you know from what you suspect. Say
  when you are unsure. Getting a value right matters more than being right.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Expertise is welcome from anyone, whatever their background or seniority.
  A good correction from a first-time contributor is exactly what this project is for.
- **Keep the scope honest.** Do not represent a reproducibility result as a claim about biological
  correctness or clinical validity. Do not use this project to make medical claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, and any conduct that
would make a reasonable contributor feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, issues, and comments that violate this
code, and may block repeat or serious offenders. To report a concern, contact the maintainer at
**hi@claygood.com**. Reports will be handled discreetly.

This code applies in all project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project in
public.
